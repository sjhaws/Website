# Space image credits

All 14 space images come from NASA's Image and Video Library ([images.nasa.gov](https://images.nasa.gov)). NASA imagery is generally not copyrighted; credit is requested. Each was cropped square around its subject and resized to 360 px WebP. Vesta's "40 km" scale bar was blacked out.

Hard mode shows these credits under the board. They're listed in `src/games/memory-game/faces.ts`, so update both together.
